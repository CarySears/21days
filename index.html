What does a small act of self-care actually look like?

Sometimes the simplest invitations are the ones we most need.

Here are three examples from the course.

Take five minutes to breathe

Set a timer, put your phone aside, and give yourself five uninterrupted minutes to simply breathe.

Anna provides a guided breathing track to accompany you.

No multitasking. Nothing to accomplish.

Just a few minutes to be with yourself.

Clear one small space

Choose a drawer, a shelf, or even the inside of your bag.

Take everything out, clean the space, and decide what belongs there.

Finish what you started, and take a moment to appreciate what you've done.

The practice isn't about having a perfect home. It's about giving yourself the experience of starting, finishing, and making a little room.

Listen to your heart

Place a hand over your heart and take a gentle breath.

Ask yourself:

How can I love myself today?

What are three ways I can care for myself?

Then give yourself a moment to listen.

You don't have to force an answer.

Sometimes the act of asking is a meaningful place to begin.

A little about me

Hi, I'm Anna Brooke.

I've been a breathworker since 2010, and over the years my work has continued to bring me back to one essential relationship: the one I have with myself.

But I didn't always understand that.

For a long time, my attention was focused outward, toward other people, their feelings, their needs, and their expectations.

It took years of learning, practicing, and paying attention to realize that caring for myself wasn't a luxury.

It was a necessity.

And that realization changed how I approached my life.

I originally created this course in 2020, during a time when so many people were facing uncertainty, isolation, and a profound shift in their everyday lives.

I kept thinking about the helpers, the caregivers, and the people who were so busy holding everything together.

Who was taking care of them?

That question stayed with me.

And while the world has changed since then, the importance of caring for ourselves hasn't.

I created these 21 days to share some of the practices, reflections, and lessons that have helped me develop a more loving relationship with myself.

Not because I have everything figured out.

But because I know how meaningful it can be to begin showing up for yourself in small, consistent ways.

“The most important relationship I will ever have is the one I have with myself.”

I'd love to help you explore what that relationship can look like for you.

Anna x
