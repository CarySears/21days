Your 21-day self-care journey

The course unfolds over three weeks, moving from awareness and breath into clearing, letting go, and reconnecting with your heart.

Here's a glimpse of what we'll explore together.

Days 1–7: Notice, breathe, and move

We begin with awareness.

What gives you energy? What takes it away? How do your thoughts and words shape the way you experience each day?

We'll explore the difference between caring for yourself and soothing yourself, then begin putting that awareness into practice through breath and movement.

You'll explore:

  • Noticing what energizes and depletes you.
  • The power of thoughts, words, and affirmations.
  • Self-care versus self-soothing.
  • Connecting with your breath.
  • A guided five-minute breathing practice.
  • Moving your body simply for the pleasure of it.

The invitation: Begin paying attention to yourself with curiosity rather than judgment.

Days 8–14: Make space, inside and out

In our second week, we'll explore how our surroundings affect us, and how small acts of clearing can become meaningful acts of self-care.

We'll practice making ourselves a priority, surrendering what lies outside our control, and discovering what can happen when we start and finish one small thing.

You'll explore:

  • Gentle stretching with Anna.
  • Making yourself a priority.
  • Surrendering what you cannot control.
  • Clearing and organizing one small space at a time.
  • The feelings and memories attached to our belongings.
  • Practicing letting go.
  • Your living space as a reflection of your inner world.

The invitation: Give yourself permission to make room for what matters.

Days 15–21: Return to your body and heart

As we approach the end of our time together, we'll slow down.

After exploring awareness, movement, and letting go, we'll turn toward rest, gratitude, and the relationship you have with yourself.

You'll explore:

  • Taking a well-deserved break.
  • A writing practice of releasing, inviting, and gratitude.
  • Returning to your body through gentle movement.
  • Taking one small action to care for yourself.
  • Listening to your heart.
  • Reflecting on love, guidance, and what matters to you.
  • Closing our journey with a simple prayer of gratitude.

The invitation: Carry the practice of caring for yourself into the days ahead.

A personal note from Anna

“The most important relationship I will ever have is the one I have with myself.”

It took me a long time to understand just how true those words are.

When I was younger, I put so much of my attention on other people: their feelings, their needs, their opinions of me.

I hadn't really learned how to show up for myself.

Over time, through my own experiences and my work as a breathworker since 2010, I began to understand that caring for myself wasn't a frivolity.

It was a necessity.

And that learning continues.

I created the first version of this course in 2020, when the world suddenly became a very uncertain place.

I kept thinking about all the helpers. The caregivers. The people tending to everyone around them.

And I wondered: who was taking care of them?

That question feels just as relevant today.

Especially at this time of year, when so much of our attention goes toward others, our responsibilities, and everything we hope to accomplish before January arrives.

I wanted to create a gentle reminder that you belong in the circle of care, too.

That's what these 21 days are about.

Not becoming better at doing everything.

But learning to show up for yourself, one small action at a time.

I'd love to share this journey with you.
