Does your fire feel low as the year closes?

This stretch of the calendar asks a lot. Gatherings, expectations, year-end deadlines, and the pressure to feel festive all at once. Many of us arrive at the finish line running on embers.

Your light has not gone out. It may just need tending.

Listen for the call to slow down, take stock of the year you have lived, and give your attention back to yourself. That is where kindling the light within begins.

Four ways to meet this season with care

Honor the year you had

The wins, the losses, and everything in between. Pause long enough to acknowledge what you carried before you step into what comes next.

Protect your peace

Insulate yourself from holiday stress and the moods and opinions swirling around you. Practice the small boundaries that keep your inner space calm.

Protect your time

Choose one priority at a time instead of juggling all of them. Your attention is a form of energy, and you get to decide where it goes.

Tend your inner fire

Breathe, move, clear a little space, and listen to your heart. Simple daily acts that rekindle the light within.

What is inside the 21 days

Part one: Ground

Days 1 to 7
  • Notice where your energy goes
  • See how thoughts and words shape your day
  • Learn self-care versus self-soothing
  • Come home to your breath
  • Move your body for the pleasure of it

Part two: Clear

Days 8 to 14
  • Stretch with a gentle practice from Anna
  • Make yourself the one priority
  • Surrender and sanitize
  • Clear one small space at a time
  • Let go, and see your home as a mirror

Part three: Kindle

Days 15 to 21
  • Take a well-deserved break
  • Release, invite, and give thanks
  • Return to your body
  • Listen to your heart
  • Close with gratitude and a prayer

A buffer for the holidays. A fresh start for the new year.

Now, as the season heats up

Reach for a few minutes of breathing, a boundary, or a single priority when the gatherings, expectations, and to-do lists pile up.

In the in-between

The days between the holidays and the new year feel quiet and strange. Use them to honor the year you had and clear a little space, one drawer at a time.

Into January

Begin the year from a steadier place. Not a list of resolutions, but a reflex of care you have already been building. Return to any day whenever you need it.
